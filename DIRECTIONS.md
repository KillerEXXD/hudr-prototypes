# Scout — visual bake-off directions

Five light/modern visual directions over the **same** Scout Engine app (identical
features, flow, and data presentation — only the visual identity changes). Each is a
separate build/card on the gallery; the dark functional original stays at
`/scout-engine-demo/`.

All five add the brief's shared treatments: the **"vs typical" context bar** (value
plotted against the typical population band with a confidence-colored marker),
**confidence-as-opacity** (tentative stats de-emphasized + "small sample" tag, noise
hidden), and **warm "opportunity" leak colors** (never alarm-red).

| Direction | Palette | Type | Best for | Risk taken |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Clarivue** | Light `#FAFBFC`, blue `#2563EB`, warm leak `#E8603C` | Inter | Everyone — the cohesive default | None; it's the control, made impeccable |
| **Terminal** | Light `#F7F8FA`, indigo `#4F46E5` | IBM Plex Sans + **Plex Mono** numbers | Grinders who think in numbers | Pushed density; added a Compact/Comfortable toggle |
| **Felt** | Warm off-white `#F6F4EF`, deep-felt `#1B5E47` + hairline gold `#B08A3E` | Fraunces serif headline + Inter | Brand/premium feel; the report-as-dossier | The gold — kept to a hairline |
| **Pulse** | `#FBFAFC`, violet `#7C5CFC`, coral `#FF6B5C` | Plus Jakarta Sans | Recreational players who screenshot/share | Warmth without becoming toy-like; hero exploit cards |
| **Edge** | White, near-black `#101010`, one vermilion `#FF4D2E` | Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono | Opinionated, editorial brand | Near-monochrome — one accent carries only the top leak |

## My recommendation
**Clarivue** for the pro-core-plus-rec-report goal. It's the only one that serves
both audiences without compromise: the dense stat table with the vs-typical bar reads
as trustworthy to a grinder, while the same calm shell makes the plain-English report
genuinely screenshot-able for a rec player. It's also the most future-proof base — the
neutral SaaS palette lets the **archetype identity colors** and **warm leak accents**
do the semantic work without fighting a strong brand hue.

- If the audience skews **pro**, go **Terminal** (mono numbers + density earn trust fastest).
- If it skews **rec/social**, go **Pulse** (hero exploit cards are the most shareable).
- **Felt** is the strongest *brand* statement; **Edge** the most distinctive but the most opinionated.

## Note
The hand replayer keeps its green-felt table inside every theme — a poker table is
green regardless of app chrome, so it's intentionally left as-is.
