ClubR Referrals — How It Works

Players invite players. When someone you referred buys coins with real money, you earn a share of that purchase — a lot in year one, tapering but continuing for life. You also earn a flat 10% residual on everyone they refer. It's uncapped revenue sharing — configurable, and locked in the day it starts.

Earnings come only from marketplace coin purchases — nothing else. Free promo coins never count.

Referrer (A) Direct — 50 / 30 / 20% Residual — flat 10% on all of B's referrals Coin $ (real purchase) App keeps the rest
1.

The idea, in one line

A referrer earns a percentage of the coin money their invitees spend — generous early, tapering over time, but never stopping. On top of that, they earn a flat 10% on every purchase made by everyone their invitee refers. A few guardrails keep it fair; the ceiling is a setting, currently switched off (No Cap).

💵 Paid out as real, withdrawable cash ✅ Only real paid purchases count — free promo coins never do 📉 Direct: 50% → 30% → 20% by year, then 20% for life 🔁 Residual: flat 10% on all of your invitee's referrals 🧱 Depth stops at 2 levels ♾️ No cap — unlimited, for life (configurable) ↩️ Refunds & chargebacks claw the earning back ⚙️ Every rate is configurable per player

Attribution locks the moment someone signs up with your code. The payout clock starts when they make their first real purchase. All figures below are the coin-purchase split before payment-processing fees.

3.

The rate tapers by year

The clock for a referred player starts at their first real purchase. Your direct cut of everything they buy steps down each year, then holds at 20% for life. The residual 10% (on all of their referrals) stays flat, for life.

🔒 Rates are locked at the start

The percentages that apply to a relationship are frozen the day it activates (first purchase). If you later change the defaults, existing referrals keep their locked rates — only new ones use the new numbers. Each player's dashboard always shows the rate that's locked for them.

⚙️ Configurable per player

50 / 30 / 20 and the 10% residual are defaults. An admin can set a different schedule for a specific referrer — e.g. a 70% year-one rate for a key partner, or 40% for another. Whatever is set at activation is what locks in.

5.

No cap — unlimited revenue sharing

configurable · currently OFF

Revenue sharing has no ceiling. As long as a referred player and their referrals keep buying coins, the referrer keeps earning — for life, uncapped. Whether a cap applies at all is a configuration the admin controls; it is currently set to No Cap.

⚙️ App Setting · Referral Cap
✓
No Cap
Revenue share continues for life with no ceiling — direct + residual, unlimited. This is the active setting.
Selected
Cap per referral branch
Optional ceiling (e.g. $1,000) on a direct referral + its residuals, if you ever want to bound the liability.
Off

Flip this in the admin app any time. Turning a cap on later would apply going forward; already-locked relationships keep their terms.

What "No Cap" means

A strong referrer with an active downline can earn indefinitely. There's no $1,000 stop — a single relationship can pay out well beyond that over its lifetime.

Trade-off worth naming: uncapped + for-life is an unbounded, growing liability for the app. The config switch means we can cap later without a code change if the numbers demand it.

♾️ Unlimited upside for referrers 📈 Unbounded liability for the app 🔀 Reversible via one setting

11.

For discussion — what we want your read on

This is a proposal, not a final decision. A few calls have real trade-offs — tell us where you land:

1.
No cap — comfortable? Uncapped, for-life revenue share is the most attractive pitch for referrers, but it's an unbounded, growing liability for the app: a strong referrer with an active downline earns forever. The cap is a config we can turn on later — do we launch No Cap and watch the numbers, or set a generous ceiling from day one?
2.
Cash vs. coins. Paying real, withdrawable cash is the most motivating — but for a poker-adjacent app it likely needs tax reporting (W-9 / 1099), identity checks, and a legal review, since our coins are deliberately "non-cash." Would coins/credits instead be enough of an incentive, and much simpler?
3.
Margin reality. If coins are bought via the App Store / Google Play, the store takes ~30% before we see a cent. Direct 50% (Yr 1) + a 10% residual + no cap can push payouts past what we keep. Should referral-eligible purchases be web-checkout only?
4.
Transparency. Should players see the app's earnings on their referred revenue (full transparency), or just their own?
5.
Fraud. With uncapped cash on the line, what guardrails feel right — withdrawal hold periods, identity verification, device/payment de-duplication to stop fake self-referrals?
