ClubR Referrals — How It Works

Players invite players. When someone you referred buys coins with real money, you earn a share of that purchase — a lot in year one, tapering but continuing for life. Refer a chain and you earn a small residual one level down too. Every rate is configurable and locked in the day it starts.

Earnings come only from marketplace coin purchases — nothing else. Free promo coins never count.

Referrer (A) Direct — 50 / 30 / 20% Residual — flat 10% Coin $ (real purchase) App keeps the rest
1.

The idea, in one line

A referrer earns a percentage of the coin money their invitees spend. The percentage is generous early and tapers over time, and there's a second, smaller cut from the people your invitee refers. A handful of guardrails keep it fair and bounded:

💵 Paid out as real, withdrawable cash ✅ Only real paid purchases count — free promo coins never do 📉 Direct: 50% → 30% → 20% by year, then 20% for life 🔁 Residual: flat 10% one level down 🧱 Depth stops at 2 levels 🎯 Capped at $1,000 per referral branch ↩️ Refunds & chargebacks claw the earning back ⚙️ Every rate & cap is configurable per player

Attribution locks the moment someone signs up with your code. The payout clock starts when they make their first real purchase. All figures below are the coin-purchase split before payment-processing fees.

3.

The rate tapers by year

The clock for a referred player starts at their first real purchase. Your direct cut of everything they buy steps down each year, then holds at 20% for life. The residual 10% (one level down) stays flat.

🔒 Rates are locked at the start

The percentages that apply to a relationship are frozen the day it activates (first purchase). If you later change the defaults, existing referrals keep their locked rates — only new ones use the new numbers. Each player's dashboard always shows the rate that's locked for them.

⚙️ Configurable per player

50 / 30 / 20 and the 10% residual are defaults. An admin can set a different schedule for a specific referrer — e.g. a 70% year-one rate for a key partner, or 40% for another. Whatever is set at activation is what locks in.

5.

Capped at $1,000 per branch

configurable

Each direct referral is its own branch with its own $1,000 lifetime ceiling — and that ceiling covers both your direct earnings from that person and the residual from whoever they refer. Refer more people, get more independent caps.

10.

For discussion — what we want your read on

This is a proposal, not a final decision. A few calls have real trade-offs — tell us where you land:

1.
Cash vs. coins. Paying real, withdrawable cash is the most motivating — but for a poker-adjacent app it likely needs tax reporting (W-9 / 1099), identity checks, and a legal review, since our coins are deliberately "non-cash." Would coins/credits instead be enough of an incentive, and much simpler?
2.
How generous, how long? 50/30/20 for life (capped at $1,000/branch) is aggressive. Is the year-one 50% right, or should it start lower? Is $1,000 the right ceiling?
3.
Margin reality. If coins are bought via the App Store / Google Play, the store takes ~30% before we see a cent — so a year-one chain can pay out more than we keep. Should referral-eligible purchases be web-checkout only?
4.
Transparency. Should players see the app's earnings on their referred revenue (full transparency), or just their own?
5.
Fraud. With cash on the line, what guardrails feel right — withdrawal hold periods, identity verification, device/payment de-duplication to stop fake self-referrals?
