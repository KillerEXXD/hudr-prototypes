ClubrGo Referrals — How It Works

Players invite players. When someone you referred buys coins with real money, you earn a share of that purchase — a lot in year one, tapering but continuing for life. You also earn a flat 10% residual on everyone they refer. It's uncapped revenue sharing — locked in the day it starts.

Earnings come only from marketplace coin purchases — nothing else. Free promo coins never count.

Referrer (A) Direct — 50 / 30 / 20% Residual — flat 10% on all of B's referrals Coin $ (real purchase) App keeps the rest
1.

The idea, in one line

A referrer earns a percentage of the coin money their invitees spend — generous early, tapering over time, but never stopping. On top of that, they earn a flat 10% on every purchase made by everyone their invitee refers. There is no cap — your share is unlimited, for life.

💵 Paid out as real, withdrawable cash ✅ Only real paid purchases count — free promo coins never do 📉 Direct: 50% → 30% → 20% by year, then 20% for life 🔁 Residual: flat 10% on all of your invitee's referrals 🧱 Depth stops at 2 levels ♾️ No cap — unlimited, for life ↩️ Refunds & chargebacks claw the earning back 🔒 Your rate is locked in for life

Attribution locks the moment someone signs up with your code. The payout clock starts when they make their first real purchase. All figures below are the coin-purchase split before payment-processing fees.

3.

The rate tapers by year

The clock for a referred player starts at their first real purchase. Your direct cut of everything they buy steps down each year, then holds at 20% for life. The residual 10% (on all of their referrals) stays flat, for life.

🔒 Your rate is locked at the start

The percentage for each relationship is frozen the day it activates (their first purchase) — it never drops below the published year schedule. Your dashboard always shows the exact rate locked for every person you've referred.

🌱 It grows with your network

You keep earning as the people you refer — and everyone they bring in — keep buying. A single active referral can pay you for years, with no ceiling.

4.

It stops at two levels

You earn from the people you refer (direct) and from everyone they refer (residual, flat 10%). It does not go deeper — no earnings three levels down.
