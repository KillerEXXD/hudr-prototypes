# Task scenarios

Scenario‑based (a goal + context), never step‑by‑step. Each lists a **success
criterion** (what "done" looks like) so the moderator can score pass/fail
consistently. Pull the subset that fits the participant's segment.

> Framing for every task: *"The numbers are placeholder — focus on whether you
> could get where you wanted."*

---

## A. Core: scout a player (all segments)
**Scenario:** "You're about to play against **Phil Hellmuth**. Before the game,
figure out his single biggest weakness and how you'd attack it."
- **Success:** finds the player report, identifies the top leak, and can state a
  concrete counter in their own words.
- Watch: do they find search? do they trust the read? do they notice the sample size?

## B. Core: see the evidence (all segments)
**Scenario:** "You don't believe that read. Prove it — find an actual hand where
he did this, and watch it."
- **Success:** opens the stat → hands drill (or the exploit card's hand), gets to
  a hand, and opens the Watch view.
- Watch: do they discover that stats are tappable? is "Watch" obvious?

## C. Tournament: read the room (serious / pro)
**Scenario:** "You've sat down at a final table (open the **$25K WSOP** event).
Who's the easiest player to target, and who would you avoid?"
- **Success:** uses the roster/Stats/AI to name a target + a tough player with a reason.
- Watch: do they use the AI, the stat cards, or the player reports? any dead‑ends?

## D. Tournament: standings + watch a bust (all segments)
**Scenario:** "How did this tournament end — who finished where, who won how much,
and can you watch the hand that knocked out 3rd place?"
- **Success:** finds the Results view, reads standings + payouts, opens the
  elimination hand's Watch view.

## E. Drill from the table (serious / pro)
**Scenario:** "In that event, you want **this player's** 3‑betting in *this event
specifically* — and the hands behind it."
- **Success:** goes tournament → Stats → a player → switches to / lands on the
  event scope → drills the 3‑bet stat → sees the hands.
- Watch: is the scope (Career vs This event) understood? confusing?

## F. Ask the AI (all segments)
**Scenario:** "Without reading the whole page, just *ask* — who at this table
calls down too light?"
- **Success:** finds and uses an Ask‑AI surface and gets a usable answer.
- Watch: did they notice AI existed? did the answer feel trustworthy or canned?

## G. (scout‑sharp only) Trust calibration (pro)
**Scenario:** "Find this player's bet‑sizing tendencies. How much would you rely
on what you see?"
- **Success:** reaches the (estimated) bet‑sizing section and articulates that
  it's estimated/derived, not measured.
- Watch: does the "Estimated" tag land? does it help or hurt overall trust?

## H. (engine/crisp/stats) Switch the skin (optional)
**Scenario:** "Make the app look the way you'd want it to."
- **Success:** finds the skin picker and changes it.

---

## Comparative preference task (unmoderated, Phase 1)
Show the same task (**A — scout a player**) on 2–4 variants in rotated order, then:
- "Which one would you actually use? Why?"
- "Which felt easiest? Which looked most trustworthy?"
- "Anything one had that the others should steal?"
