ClubrGO — Flow Diagram

Interactive explainer • Poker clubs

A visual walkthrough of how ClubrGO works, for a product review. ClubrGO is a transparent scorekeeper for a poker club's games — FT Fantasy (predict a HUDR live‑streamed Final Table, auto‑resolved) and Last Longer (the club's own live tournament, host‑judged + audit log). The app proves the count & result but awards/holds/touches nothing — clubs settle the cash offline. Covers the dishonest‑host problem it solves, roles, by‑role pricing, the legal “wall,” and the data model.
